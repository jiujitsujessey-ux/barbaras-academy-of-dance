Please note

Tuition is based on a 9-month season and is billed in nine equal monthly payments, posted on the 1st of each month from September 1 through May 1. Classes run September 14 through June 14; the May payment covers the final weeks of the season, so there is no June payment.

Payment is due on the 1st of the month. Accounts not paid by the 10th are assessed a $15 late fee. A returned check or declined card is assessed a $30 fee.

We accept cash, checks, and credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Discover). We do not accept American Express.

Once you have completed online registration, we will adjust your tuition rate.
