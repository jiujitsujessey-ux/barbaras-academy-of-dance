Why it matters

Some studios put on a show. Ours raises a cast.

The Nutcracker is where everything the studio teaches comes together — technique, artistry, and being part of something bigger than yourself. Roles grow with your dancer: this year's tiny angel is a future Clara, and this year's Clara is a future Sugar Plum.

Look closely at the cast photo and you'll spot something we love: it isn't just students up there. Parents, staff, and grown dancers share the stage — because at a 40-year-old studio, The Nutcracker is a family affair in every sense.

  • Every other Decembera biennial tradition, performed for the community
  • A role for every agefrom first-year angels to the Performing Company
  • Directed by Miss BeBefounder of our award-winning company
