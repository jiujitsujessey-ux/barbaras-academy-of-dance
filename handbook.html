Welcome

Welcome to Barbara’s Academy of Dance. For 40 years we have trained dancers ages 3 to adult in Farmington Hills with the warmth of a small studio and the standards of a serious one. This handbook covers the everyday policies that keep classes running smoothly.

Tuition & fees

  • Tuition is based on a 9-month season, September through June, and is billed monthly — the same amount each month regardless of how many classes fall in that month.
  • Tuition is set by class length and by how many classes your dancer takes — see the current price list at barbarasdancestudio.com. Multi-class and family discounts apply automatically.
  • Tuition is due the 1st of each month. A $15 late fee applies to accounts unpaid after the 10th.
  • Registration fee: $40 per family, plus $15 for each additional child. Non-refundable.
  • Payment is made by credit or debit card in your Jackrabbit parent portal, where you can also set up autopay.
  • Recital costume deposits are due in late October — $85 per class, Trio $180, Combo $90.

Attendance & make-up classes

  • Please report absences by calling or texting the studio at (248) 476-7074, or by emailing barbarasdance2@gmail.com.
  • Missed classes may be made up in a comparable class within four weeks, when space allows. There are no refunds or tuition credits for missed classes.
  • Consistent attendance matters most in the months before the recital, when choreography is set.

Weather closures

If Farmington Public Schools close for weather, the studio generally closes as well. Closures are announced by email and on our Facebook page — when in doubt, call (248) 476-7074. Weather closures are treated as absences eligible for make-up; tuition is not prorated.

Arrival & pick-up

  • Please arrive 5–10 minutes early so dancers can change shoes and be ready when class starts.
  • Young dancers must be walked into the lobby and picked up inside — please don't wait in the parking lot.
  • Tell the front desk if someone new is picking up your dancer.

Class attire

Every style has its own dress code — see the separate Dress Code & Attire Policy on our website at barbarasdancestudio.com. Hair secured off the face, no dangling jewelry, and no street shoes on studio floors, for every class.

Communication

  • Your Jackrabbit parent portal holds your dancer's schedule, tuition, balance, and payments — reach it from barbarasdancestudio.com → Portal.
  • Studio news, closures, and reminders come by email, so please keep your address current in the portal.
  • Email: barbarasdance2@gmail.com · Phone: (248) 476-7074.

Withdrawal

Enrollment continues for the full season, September through June, unless you tell us otherwise. Tuition is billed in nine monthly payments, September 1 through May 1.

To drop a class or withdraw a dancer, notify the studio in writing — email to barbarasdance2@gmail.com is fine — at least 30 days before the next billing date. Tuition continues to be charged until we receive written notice. Telling an instructor in class is not sufficient.

Tuition already paid is not refunded, and monthly tuition is not prorated for a partial month. The registration fee is non-refundable.

Costume, competition, and recital fees are non-refundable once the order has been placed. Recital costumes are ordered in January; a dancer who withdraws after that remains responsible for costume costs already committed.

Dancers who withdraw after choreography is set may not be able to rejoin the recital piece, as their part will have been reassigned.

Exceptions for injury or relocation are considered case by case — please call and talk to us.
