Our studio · Since 1985

Four decades of first pliés. One studio, still growing.

Since 1985, this Farmington Hills studio has been where local kids learn their first plié and grow into confident dancers and confident people. We teach ballet, pointe, tap, jazz, lyrical, contemporary, hip-hop, and acro for ages 3 through adult. Four decades of families have called it home, and that will not change.

Today the studio is led by owner and director Barbara Bouchard, known to her students as “Miss BeBe.” In 2025, the Michigan Dance Council named her Private Sector Educator of the Year. Founder Barbara Hatch, known to generations of dancers as “Miss Barb,” built the studio and its traditions. Our faculty is a team of teachers that families know by name. Our dance family includes everyone from tiny dancers taking their first steps to company dancers preparing their Nutcracker solos, and we would love to welcome yours.

Our Team · Led by Miss BeBe

Meet Barbara Bouchard “Miss BeBe”

Dancing since she was three and trained in every style she could find (Scottish Highland included), she's been part of this studio since 2001: first as a teacher, then as founder, director, and choreographer of its Performing Company, and today as the studio's owner.

Under Miss BeBe's direction the company has earned eight national titles, and in 2025 the Michigan Dance Council named her its Private Sector Educator of the Year. With Miss Catherine, Mr. Bill, Miss Ava, and Miss Meredith teaching and Miss Kim running the office, classes start with a strong ballet foundation, and the proof is in where our students have landed.

Questions parents ask

Everything you need to know before day one.

How old does my dancer need to be to start?

We welcome dancers from age 3 in our Young Dancers classes, with leveled classes in every style through age 18.

When does the dance season run?

September through June, finishing with our year-end recital, the night every dancer works toward all season.

How do I register?

Through our online registration portal in a few minutes. Not sure which class fits? Call or email and we'll help you find the right one.

What does my dancer need for the first class?

We send everything before day one: dress code, shoes, and where to go. No need to buy anything before you hear from us.

Do you offer competitive dance?

Yes. Our audition-based Performing Company has won eight national titles and performs everywhere from our Nutcracker to the national stage.
