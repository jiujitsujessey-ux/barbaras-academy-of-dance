Questions parents ask

Good questions.

Everything you need to know before day one — and if your question isn't here, call or email and a real person answers.

Getting started

How old does my dancer need to be to start?

We welcome dancers from age 3 in our preschool combo classes, with leveled classes in every style through age 18 — plus adult classes.

How do I register?

Through our online registration page in a few minutes, or call (248) 476-7074 and Miss Kim will help you find the right class.

When does the dance season run?

September through June, finishing with our year-end recital. Summer camps and intensives run in July and August.

What should my dancer wear to class?

Each style has a simple dress code. New families receive everything before day one — no need to buy anything until you hear from us.

Classes & placement

What styles of dance do you teach?

Ballet, pointe, tap, jazz, lyrical, contemporary, hip-hop, and acro — plus preschool combo classes that mix styles for our youngest dancers. See the full genre guide.

How is my dancer placed in a class?

By age and experience. Most classes are grouped by age range with leveled options as dancers advance — call the office and we'll place your dancer in the right spot.

What if we miss a class?

Report the absence in our Parent Portal or call the office. To make up a missed class, check with the front desk — if a comparable class has room, we'll fit your dancer in.

Do you offer adult classes?

Yes — the schedule includes adult classes. Call for current offerings.

Registration

Can I register mid-season?

Often, yes — if there's room in the right class. Call the office and we'll check availability for your dancer.

Performances

Is there a recital?

Yes — every dancer works toward our year-end recital in June, the night the whole season builds to. We also stage The Nutcracker every other December.

Do you offer competitive dance?

Yes. Our audition-based Performing Company has won eight national titles and performs everywhere from our Nutcracker to the national stage.

Day to day

What is the Parent Portal?

A free account for enrolled families — class confirmations, the season calendar, absence reporting, and studio news in one place. Join here.

How do I hear about studio news and closings?

Watch your email and the Parent Portal — and follow along on Instagram and Facebook.

Still wondering?

Ask us anything.

No question is too small — call, email, or send a note from the contact page and a real person answers.
